Stockfish 18 (lite, single-threaded WASM)
Copied from the npm package stockfish@18.0.8, files bin/stockfish-18-lite-single.{js,wasm}.
Also shipped as stockfish.js / stockfish.wasm so the Worker URL and the Emscripten
.locateFile basename stay aligned (the glue resolves .wasm from its own script name).
Single-threaded on purpose: a multi-threaded build needs SharedArrayBuffer, which needs
COOP and COEP response headers that this app's hosting does not send.
