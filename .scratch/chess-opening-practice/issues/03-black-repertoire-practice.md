**Blocked by:** 02 — White repertoire practice

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Classical Caro-Kann course uses the agreed Black core line, three levels, original explanations, and roughly 8–12 positions per lesson.
- [ ] The board clearly identifies Black-side practice and presents positions from Black’s perspective where appropriate.

- [ ] The dashboard shows both Black courses with side, progress, and next available lesson.
- [ ] Black-course completion, attempts, missed positions, unlocks, and review state persist to the owner’s Firestore data.
